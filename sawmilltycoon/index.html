Cut trees, load the log trucks and turn wood into furniture in this 3D idle lumber tycoon!
Sawmill Tycoon: Wood Factory lets you build a logging camp, run a busy sawmill and watch your deliveries build a whole town.

FROM FOREST TO FACTORY
- Lumberjacks with chainsaws and crane harvesters cut the trees
- Log trucks drive the logs to your yard on a one-way road
- Forklifts carry goods between the machines
- Log splitter, sawmill, chair bench, table lathe and cabin works
- Turn logs into planks, firewood, chairs, tables and cabin kits
- Buyer lorries pull into the bays, load up and drive away

120 UNIQUE CAMPS
- 8 forests: Pine Valley, Birch Meadow, Maple Hills, Snowy Spruce, Redwood Canyon, Cherry Blossom, Bamboo River and Desert Oasis
- 6 camp layouts, mirrored and mixed, so no two camps play the same
- Special camps: a beaver that steals logs, free logs on the river, rush hour, rain, big orders and a golden tree
- Finish a camp to win the golden axe and move to a new forest

BUILD THE TOWN
Every delivery helps build the town across the road. Each camp has 6 houses to finish, and every finished house raises all your prices.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
- A new goal is always waiting, with a cash reward
- Rush orders with a timer pay gold coins
- Daily goal with a streak
- Speed and double cash boosts
- Your crew keeps working while you are away, up to 4 hours at full rate

UPGRADE EVERYTHING
- Stations up to level 40
- Faster trucks and forklifts that carry more
- More customers with shop upgrades

Relaxing idle play with real vehicles, soft sounds and a clean 3D world. Drag to look around your camp and pinch to zoom.
