Privacy Policy

Steel Valley: Tank Air Strike · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Steel Valley: Tank Air Strike mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built Steel Valley to be enjoyed without an account, without sign-up, and without giving us any personal details.

Information We Collect

We do not ask for personal details and have no servers of our own. Google services in the App (ads and analytics, described below) collect some data. Your game progress (such as completed missions, coins, stars, and garage upgrades) is saved on your own device.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

From the November 2026 update, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played. It may collect an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, operating system version, approximate location derived from the IP address, session and diagnostic information, gameplay events such as levels started, completed or failed, ads watched and which optional in-app product was bought. Google processes this data under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Uninstalling the App stops this collection.

Advertising

The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. This includes banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for in-game bonuses. AdMob may automatically collect certain device information to serve and measure ads, such as:

  • Device identifiers (for example, the advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system version, and app usage signals related to ad delivery

This data is processed by Google under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are served through Google AdMob in accordance with applicable ad policies.

Permissions

The App only uses network access, which is required to display advertisements. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell personal information. Data handled by Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
