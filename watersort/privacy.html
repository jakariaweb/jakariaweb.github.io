Privacy Policy

Water Sort: Color Puzzle · Effective date: November 24, 2026

Water Sort: Color Puzzle ("the App") is a casual puzzle game. You can play it without creating an account, without signing in, and without giving us any personal details. This page explains what the App does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for personal details and have no servers of our own. Google services in the App (ads and analytics, described below) collect some data. Your progress (current level, unlocked levels, sound and vibration settings) is saved only on your own device.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

From the November 2026 update, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played. It may collect an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, operating system version, approximate location derived from the IP address, session and diagnostic information, gameplay events such as levels started, completed or failed, ads watched and which optional in-app product was bought. Google processes this data under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Uninstalling the App stops this collection.

Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads. Ads are delivered by a third-party advertising service, which may process standard device information (such as the advertising identifier, IP address and device type) to show and measure ads. That processing is governed by the ad partner's own policy: how our ad partner uses information. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

In-app purchase

The App offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the app store; we never see or store your payment details. After the purchase, the App shows no ads.

Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

The App uses internet access for ads, analytics and the optional purchase. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
