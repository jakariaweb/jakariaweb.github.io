Dig sparkling crystals, cut flawless gems and craft royal jewelry in this 3D idle gem mine tycoon!
Gem Mine Tycoon: Jewel Factory lets you run a busy crystal mine, fill your gem collection and watch your deliveries build a whole town.

FROM ROCK TO ROYAL JEWELRY
- Miners in hard hats break the gem veins, drill rigs help them
- Mine trains carry the ore on rails, electric gem carts move the goods
- Gold smelter, gem cutter, ring bench, necklace bench and crown works
- Turn ore into gold bars, cut gems, rings, necklaces and crowns
- Buyer trucks pull into the bays, load up and drive away

FLAWLESS GEMS AND GEODES
- Every cut gem can come out Flawless: it glows and sells for 5x
- Upgrade the gem cutter for more flawless cuts
- Miners dig up geodes: tap 3 times to crack them open for coins or rare gems
- Geodes are free, never sold for money

GEM COLLECTION
- 48 gems to collect, from Rough to Legendary
- Every gem you find raises all your prices, every full world page raises them more
- Royal orders with a timer pay gold coins and sometimes a rare gem

120 UNIQUE MINES IN 8 WORLDS
- Emerald Hills, Topaz Canyon, Sapphire Ice, Ruby Volcano, Jade Ruins, Aqua Reef, Amethyst Cave and Diamond Peak
- Different layouts, products and twists in every mine: a mole that steals ore, a free ore conveyor, rush hour, rich season, big orders and a giant crystal
- Veins dig deeper every 10 levels for richer ore
- Finish a mine to win the golden pickaxe and move on

BUILD THE TOWN
Every delivery helps build the town across the road. Each mine has 6 houses to finish, and every finished house raises your prices.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
- A new goal is always waiting, with a cash reward
- Daily goal with a streak, speed and double cash boosts
- Your miners keep working while you are away, up to 4 hours at full rate
