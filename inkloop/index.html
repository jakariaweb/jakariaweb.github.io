Pixel shooter puzzle: pop pixel art pictures color by color. 2,000 levels.

Tap an ink shooter, send it around the belt and watch it pop every pixel of its color. Inkloop is a pixel shooter puzzle with 2,000 levels and 300+ pixel art pictures.

Every level is a pixel art puzzle: a picture made of glossy colored pixels sits inside a conveyor belt. Pick the right shooters in the right order and peel the picture layer by layer, from the outside in. It plays like a color sort puzzle where planning matters, because belt slots are limited.

HOW TO PLAY
- Tap a shooter at the front of a queue to send it onto the belt.
- It rides around the picture and pops the outermost pixels of its own color, one ink per pixel.
- Empty shooters leave. Shooters with ink left wait in a belt slot, and you can tap them to go again.
- Clear the whole picture to win. If every slot is full and nothing can pop, the level is lost.

WHAT'S INSIDE

- Extra slot, Shuffle and Bomb boosters
- Daily challenge with a calendar and streaks
- Level map with worlds, stars and coins
- Play offline, anytime

If you enjoy color sort games, pixel sort puzzles or a good conveyor puzzle, Inkloop gives you a fresh pixel shooter puzzle every day. Think ahead, choose your colors and clear the picture.
