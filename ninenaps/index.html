Place one cat in every row, every column and every color, and never let two cats touch. A calm cat logic puzzle with 1,500 solver-checked levels.

Nine Naps is a cozy brain logic puzzle in the style of queens and star battle puzzles, with sleepy cats instead of crowns. Every grid has exactly one solution, and you can always find it by logic. No guessing needed.

HOW TO PLAY
- Put exactly one cat in each row, each column and each colored region.
- Cats need their space: no two cats may touch, not even diagonally.
- Tap a cell to mark it with X, double tap to place a cat.
- You have 3 lives, so think before you place.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 1,500 cat logic puzzle levels, from small 5x5 grids to big 12x12 boards
- Every level is checked by a solver, so every puzzle has one clear solution
- New ideas as you go: bushes, sleeping cats, paw prints that count cats, and foggy cells
- Hard levels with bonus coins
- Daily puzzle with a calendar, streaks and a leaderboard
- Hint, Auto X and Extra life boosters
- Cute sleepy cats that wake up and dance when you solve a grid
- Play offline, anytime
- Achievements and leaderboards with Google Play Games
- Colorblind mode, sound, music and haptics settings

Love queens puzzles, star battle or cat sudoku? Nine Naps gives you the same clean logic with calm colors and happy cats. Train your brain a few minutes a day and see how far your streak goes.
