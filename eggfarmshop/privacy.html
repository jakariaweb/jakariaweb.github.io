Egg Farm Shop: Idle Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Developer: YH TEAM · Effective date: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Egg Farm Shop: Idle Tycoon ("the App") handles information. There is no registration, no login screen and we never ask for your name or email. The App works fully offline.

Game progress (local and cloud saves)

Your progress (farm progress, cash, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may include it in your own Google backup. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also stored in your own Play Games cloud save so that you can restore it on another device. We have no servers and receive no copy.

Google Play Games Services

When Google Play Games is available, the App signs you in automatically using your existing Play Games profile to offer achievements, leaderboards and cloud save. Scores and achievements you earn are sent to Google Play Games and, depending on your Play Games settings, may be visible to other players. The App does not read your email address or contacts. You can turn this off in the Play Games app, and the App keeps working without it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect the advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system version, app interactions, diagnostics and ad interaction data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your choice before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy options.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played. It may collect an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, operating system version, approximate location derived from the IP address, session and diagnostic information, and gameplay events such as levels or upgrades started and completed, boosters used, ads watched and which optional product was bought.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers optional purchases: "Remove ads", "Double income", a one-time "Starter bundle" and coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details and only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.

In-app review

The App may show the Google Play rating window. Reviews are handled by Google Play.

Permissions

Internet access (ads, analytics, purchases, Play Games), vibration (haptic feedback), advertising ID, and, only if you turn on the reminder, notifications about your farm shop. Reminders are created on your device. The App does not use your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing, security and retention

We do not sell personal information. The Google services named above process data under Google's policies and protect it in transit with encryption. Analytics data is kept for the limited period set by Google Analytics.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes the local save. You can delete Play Games data in your Google account (Play Games app, Settings, Delete Play Games account and data). To ask us to delete analytics data, email jakariahossainweb@gmail.com with the subject "Egg Farm Shop data deletion"; we may ask for your app instance details to find it.

Changes and contact
