Run your own egg farm with one finger! Collect eggs, stock the shelves and grow a small shop into a busy farm business.

Move your farmer with a simple joystick. Pick up eggs from the hens, carry the stack on your back, fill the shelves and serve customers at the counter. Collect the cash, then stand on a pad to buy the next upgrade.

- Watch the patience bars and keep customers happy
- Serve VIP customers who pay 3x

A FARM TYCOON WITH PLACES TO GO
- 60 farms in 6 settings: village, beach, snow, desert, city and tropical
- Daily goal with a streak
- Boosts for the busy moments
- Offline earnings for up to 4 hours
- 16 languages
- Optional Google Play Games achievements, leaderboards and cloud save

Play it as an egg shop idle game with helpers doing the work, or run every shelf yourself. Your egg farm earns while you are away, so there is cash to collect when you come back.
