Block Puzzle: Blast Blocks Privacy Policy

Developer: YHUB SOFT · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Block Puzzle: Blast Blocks mobile game ("the App", "we", "us"), developed by YHUB SOFT, handles information. The App has no accounts, no sign-up and no login, and we do not ask you for any personal details.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers; the App has no servers of its own. Your game progress (scores, levels, coins, medals and settings) is saved on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this game data in your own Google account backup, which is handled by Google, not by us.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

From the November 2026 update, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played. It may collect an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device model, operating system version, approximate location derived from the IP address, session and diagnostic information, gameplay events such as levels started, completed or failed, ads watched and which optional in-app product was bought. Google processes this data under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Uninstalling the App stops this collection.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, and ad interaction data (for example, ad views and clicks)

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

Consent for Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time from the App's Settings screen using the Privacy options button.

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers an optional "Remove Ads" purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so the App can remove ads.

Notifications and Permissions

The App can show an optional daily reminder notification. It is scheduled on your device and can be turned off in the App's Settings or in Android settings. The App uses internet access for ads, analytics and Google Play purchases, and vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell personal information. Data handled by Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Data Deletion

All game data is stored on your device: uninstalling the App or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Block Puzzle: Blast Blocks → Storage → Clear storage) deletes it. To reset advertising data, reset your advertising ID in your device settings. If you have any other request about your data, email us at the address below and we will help.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
