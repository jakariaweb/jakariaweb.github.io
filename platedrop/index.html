Tap a screw, send it to the box of its color and watch the plates drop. A calm screw color sort puzzle with 1,500 levels, each one checked by a solver.

Plate Drop is a nuts and bolts puzzle where colored screws pin layered plates to a board. Unscrew them in the right order, fill the color boxes and clear the board. Plan ahead: a screw under a higher plate stays stuck, and the holding slots fill up fast.

HOW TO PLAY
- Tap a screw to send it into the box of the same color at the top.
- Two boxes show at a time and each one takes 3 screws. Full boxes fly away and new ones arrive.
- A screw under a higher plate cannot come out. Clear the plate on top first.
- When all its screws are out, a plate drops.
- No box for that color yet? The screw waits in a holding slot. If the slots are full and no move is left, the level is lost.
