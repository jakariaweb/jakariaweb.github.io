Drag each colored block out through the gate of its color and clear the board. A smart slide block puzzle with 5,000 solver-checked levels.

Gate Slide is a color block escape game for anyone who loves a clean block jam puzzle. Every block slides along the grid until a wall, a stone or another block stops it. Line it up with a gate of the same color, make sure it fits, and watch it leave the board. Clear every block to win.

HOW TO PLAY
- Drag a block. It slides along the grid until something stops it.
- A block leaves the board through a gate of its own color, if it fits the gate width.
- Blocks come in many shapes, so plan the order of your moves.
- Clear all blocks to win the level. From level 11, beat the clock!

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 5,000 slide block puzzle levels, every one checked by a solver
- The same level number is the same puzzle for every player
- 10 relaxed tutorial levels with no timer
- Every 10th level is a hard level with bonus coins
- New mechanics as you go: ice blocks with counters, stones, keys and chained blocks, arrow blocks that move only one way, and gates that switch color
- Boards up to 8x10 with up to 18 blocks
- Freeze, Hammer and +30 seconds boosters
- Daily challenge with a calendar and streaks
- Level map with worlds, stars and coins
- Play offline, anytime
- Achievements and leaderboards with Google Play Games
- Colorblind mode, plus sound, music, haptics and notification settings

Like a color block puzzle that makes you think? In Gate Slide every move counts: open the way, slide smart and empty the grid before time runs out. One more slide block puzzle is always waiting.
