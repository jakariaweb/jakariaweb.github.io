Picture Car Jam: Parking Sort

Tap cars out of picture levels and fill them by color in an offline puzzle.

Get it on Google Play
Every level is a picture made of cars. Clear the road, pick the right color and solve the car jam!

Hearts, cats, butterflies, rockets, letters: over 350 pictures are built from small colored cars with arrows. Tap a car. If its road is clear, it drives out to a parking slot. Waiting passengers board the car of their color, and full cars drive off. Choose the order well: when every slot is full and nobody can board, the level is lost.

HOW THIS CAR JAM WORKS
- Read the arrows and find a car with a free road
- Fill 5 parking slots, up to 7 with extra slots
- Match cars to passengers, like a bus jam color sort
- Empty the whole picture to win

WHAT YOU GET
- 5,000 levels of traffic puzzle play
- Cars 1 to 3 cells long
- Mystery cars, frozen cars, locked cars with keys and hidden passengers
- Boosters: Shuffle, Sort line, Extra slot and Helicopter
- Five worlds: city, snow village, beach, farm and night city
- Daily challenge with a calendar and streak
- Star ratings on your levels
- Colorblind symbols and 16 languages
- Optional Google Play Games achievements, leaderboards and cloud save
